Sub: Regularization of Attendance Records and its related transactions in SAP ERP System

With the objective to contain the pandemic situation arising out of COVID-19 outbreak in the country certain stipulations/ restrictions on enforcement of 'Complete Safety Restrictions' were circulated by the Govt. from time to time effecting Lockdown w.e.f 23.03.2020. Accordingly, the Company has also regulated its business activities through issuance of necessary Circulars/Office Orders periodically maintaining parity with the Govt. guidelines.

By virtue of this, the Company has also relaxed the norms on SAP-ERP related activities (HCM Module) in the context of payroll processing of its employees by exempting the provision of recording of Attendance Records in SAP-ERP system for three consecutive payroll months i.e April-2020, May-2020 & June-2020 where salary was disbursed on provisional attendance basis.

Now, considering the present scenario, the Govt. has imposed few directives on unlocking of certain activities in a phased manner w.e.f 01.06.2020. In the above backdrop and in view of functioning of all the offices of the company w.e.f. 16.06.2020, it has been decided to regularize the payroll related activities which were kept pending so long through uploading of attendance records (16.03.2020 to 15.06.2020), regularization of the absence period and performing its related transactions like disbursement of Overtime Allowance(OTA), Night Shift Allowance (NSA), etc. for the payroll months from April-2020 to June-2020, together with transactions pertaining to the month of July-2020 payroll, so that all related adjustments in payment/leave may be settled along with the July-2020 salary. All the pending activities as aforestated need to be completed before executing leave accrual programme for the period from 01.07.2020 to 31.12.2020.
