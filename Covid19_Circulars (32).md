Circular No. CHR/06

Dated: 29.05.2021

Sub: Extension of period for following Preventive Measures to Contain Spread of COVID-19 Pandemic

Keeping in view of the Order dated 15.05.2021 issued vide No. 647-ISS/2M-22/2020 of the Chief Secretary, Govt. of West Bengal notifying certain restrictions w.e.f. 6 AM of $16^{\mathrm{th}}$ May 2021 to 6 PM of $30^{\mathrm{th}}$ May 2021 throughout the State as mentioned therein, certain stipulations/restrictions have been imposed on running of day to day activities of different units of WBSEDCL across the State up to $30^{\mathrm{th}}$ May 2021 vide this Office Circular No. CHR/03 dated 16.05.2021.

Now in view of the Order dated 29.05.2021 issued vide No. 707-ISS/2M-22/2020 of the Chief Secretary, Govt. of West Bengal, it is further notified for information of all concerned that the stipulations/restrictions imposed vide this Office Circular No. CHR/03 dated 16.05.2021 is, hereby, extended for different units of WBSEDCL throughout the State up to 6.00 PM of $15^{\mathrm{th}}$ June 2021.
