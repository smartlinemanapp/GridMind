Office Order:11

Date:28.01.2022

Consequent upon continuation of Pandemic situation in the year Calendar 2021 and the restrictions imposed from time to time towards mobilization of staff to contain spread of Novel Corona Virus (Covid'19), vide Circular Nos-26/2020 dt. 30.09.2020, CHR/06 dt. 29.01.2021, CHR/01 dt. 19.04.2021, CHR/02 dt. 30.04.2021, CHR/03 dt. 16.05.2021, CHR/06 dt. 29.05.2021, CHR/08 dt. 15.06.2021, CHR/09 dt. 30.06.2021, CHR/10 dt. 15.07.2021 and CHR/12 dt. 31.07.2021 respectively, the total working days for Calendar Year 2021 became substantially less (i.e. assessed as 183 days) than that of the working scheduled in the Calendar Year 2021 i.e. 227 days.

Such situation was prevailing even in the Calendar Year 2020 also, when the no. of working days was to be reduced and assessed minimum 122 days, circulated vide Office Order No-10 dt. 12.03.2021,to facilitate calculation of Attendance Marks i.r.o eligible employees for promotion in the Calendar Year 2021 uniformly by all concerned units of WBSEDCL.

As per the terms of Promotion Policy of the Company, the Attendance Marks of the preceding Calendar Year of the eligibility for such promotion i.r.o an incumbent is essential to assess his/her suitability for promotion to next higher post. To operationalise such provision, Cl-B of Office Order No-S/1396 dt. 08.10.2001, read with Cl-2 of Office Order No-P/125 dt. 21.06.2002 prescribes detail procedure for the purpose.

Keeping in of view such requirement & compliance of Company's rules thereof in the Pandemic Calendar Year 2021 vis-a-vis to ease the assessment of Attendance Marks for the Calendar Year 2021 uniformly for all units of WBSEDCL i.r.o. the pool of employees (upto Class-I $3^{\text{rd}}$ level) eligible for promotion in the Calendar Year 2022, it is therefore enjoined upon all concerned that no. of Working Days for the Calendar Year'2021 shall be considered atleast 183 days or actual days for which the employee concerned was assigned/asked for duty, whichever is higher.

This is for appropriate guidance & compliance of all concerned.
