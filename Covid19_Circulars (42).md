Circular No. - CHR/0b

Dated: 29.1.21

Keeping in view of the unprecedented situation due to COVID-19 pandemic and to maintain the different safety restrictions imposed by the various authorities from time to time, the offices/units [except ALDC, 33/11 KV S/Stn./Hydel Power Station/PPSP] have been functioning with $50\%$ of the total strength of the officials posted at that office on any particular day. Now, considering the relaxation of various restrictions especially restoration of public transport system, with the objective to complete certain time-bound Project works and to liquidate the mounting load of works at the this last quarter of the financial year 2020-21, the present system of attendance of the officers and staff of the Company has been reviewed and it is felt necessary to enhance the percentage of employees' attendance at their respective offices on each working day for the interest of the Company.

Now, in continuation to this Office Circulars No. 10/2020 dated 15/6/2020, 16/2020 dated 31.07.2020, 19/2020 dated 31.08.2020 and 26/2020 dated 30.09.2020 it is, hereby, notified for information of all concerned that the officers and staff posted at different offices will have to attend offices as per normal working hours on each working day (without any roster) with effect from 01st February 2021.

However, Officers and staff suffering from or developing Covid-19 symptoms may remain in home isolation with the prior permission of competent authority, as per provisions.

This has approval of the Chairman and Managing Director.
