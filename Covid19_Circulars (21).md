Circular No. - 26/2020

Dated: 30-09-2020

Sub: Preventive measures to contain the spread of COVID-19

In view of the ensuing Terminal Examination of all State aided universities to be held through online mode from 01.10.2020 to 18.10.2020 for which there is a persistent pressure of work and supervision to ensure uninterrupted supply of power during this period throughout the state and completion of project activities and making various payments in time before commencement of Puja Holidays and in continuation to this Office Circulars no. 10/2020 dated 15.06.2020, 16/2020 dated 31.07.2020 and 19/2020 dated 31.08.2020, it is further notified for information of all concerned that at the time of preparation of roster in all offices/units it should be ensured that the overall attendance of officers and staff taken together shall not exceed $50\%$ of the total strength of the officials posted at that office on any particular day, keeping Heads of Offices on duty on all working days irrespective of their ranks, as reiterated in earlier circular dt 15.06.2020 and all officials have to attend offices as per normal working hours on each working day, ie, without any shift, as per roster duty with effect from 01st October 2020.

The employees attached to ALDC in shift operation, 33/11 KV Sub-stations and Hydel Power Stations/ PPSP shall continue to perform their duty as per usual roster.

All other stipulations/restrictions as imposed vide above stated circulars shall remain in force until further order.
