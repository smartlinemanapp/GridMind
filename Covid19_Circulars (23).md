Office Circular No. CHR/

Dated: 03.01.2022

Sub: Guidelines on Preventive Measures to Contain Spread of COVID-19 Pandemic

In view of the ongoing spread of COVID-19 Pandemic and in view of the restriction order issued vide No.: 753/XVII-ISS/2M 22/ 2020 dated 02.01.2022 of the Chief Secretary, Govt. of W.B., the guidelines, as mentioned herein below, shall strictly be followed to contain the spread of COVID-19 in the workplaces of all offices of this Organisation:-

1) The attendance in any office/establishment shall not exceed $50\%$ on any given day. The employees will attend office on a rotational basis as may be decided by the Head of the Office/Controlling Officer by framing a roster of duty. However, officers of the rank of Addl. Chief Engineer, Addl. General Manager, Dy. Chief Engineer, Dy. General Manager and/or of equivalent rank and above will have to attend office regularly.   
2) At the time of preparation of roster it should be ensured that the overall attendance of officers and staff taken together shall not exceed $50\%$ of the total strength of the officials posted at the respective offices on any particular day, keeping Head of Offices on duty on all working days irrespective of their ranks and all officials will have to attend offices on normal working hours on each working day as per roster.   
3) Officers holding independent charge as Head of the office like Regional Manager, Project Manager, Divisional Manager, Station Manager and similar other officers will have to attend office on all working days as per normal working hours (without any roster).   
4) Head of HR&A and F&A wing at Zonal Offices, Regional Offices, Division Offices, Hydel H.Q., PPSP Site, Hydel Projects and other offices, irrespective of their rank, will have to attend office on all working days as per normal working hours (without any roster).   
5) Officials who will not have to attend office physically on any working day, but not on leave, have to be available over phone and/or other means of connectivity during normal office hours so that their services can be utilized as and when required.   
6) Officers/staff who are not coming to their respective offices on any day as per roster should also make themselves available in the office as per requirement of the Controlling Officer, as and when needed, in addition to their availability on electronic mode when not in office.

7) The employees attached to ALDC in shift operation, $33 / 11\mathrm{KV}$ Sub-stations and Hydel Power Stations and PPSP Site shall continue to perform their duty as per normal roster.

8) The LT and HT Mobile Vans of respective areas will function as usual for maintenance and restoration of power supply to the consumers.

9) Entrance of all offices should be provided with soaps or sanitizer for mandatory hand hygiene. All office premises should be well ventilated as far as possible.

10) While in office premises, wearing of face mask at all times, frequent hand washing, use of hand sanitizer and adherence to other safety measures as notified earlier shall be followed strictly. Any case of non-wearing of masks shall be viewed seriously.

11) Make sure that workplaces are clean and hygienic. Officers/staff will disinfect their personal equipments like keyboards, mouse, phone, AC remote etc. by themselves as frequently as possible.

12) Arrangements shall be made for disinfection of frequently touched surfaces such as electric switches, door knobs, washroom fixtures, elevator switches, etc. Arrangements should also be made for total sanitization of the entire office premises at regular intervals, i.e. once in a week.

13) Face to face meetings/discussion should be avoided as far as possible. Officers and staff will use intercom/phone/VC for interactions as far as practicable.

14) Number of people in the elevators/lifts shall be restricted. In the elevators/lifts meant for common use, not more than 3(three) persons shall be allowed to take entry at a time.

15) Entry of visitors should be restricted as far as practicable. However, sitting arrangements of visitors shall also be made at a distance of at least 2.0 metres.

16) Parisheba Mela, Safety Awareness Programme/Seminars, Training programmes shall have to be restricted and be conducted on virtual mode, as far as practicable.

17) Physical distancing of consumers shall have to be ensured by physical marking on the floor especially in front of cash counters of Customer Care Centers.

This order takes immediate effect and shall remain in force till 15.01.2022.
