Circular No: 4/2020

Dated: 27.03.2020

Sub: Preventive measures to contain the spread of COVID-19

In view of the Order dated 22.03.2020 issued vide No. H&FW/120/20 of the Chief Secretary, Govt. of West Bengal notifying "Complete Safety Restrictions" w.e.f. 16.00 hrs of 23.03.2020 to 24.00 hrs of 27.03.2020 in the urban and rural area of the State as mentioned therein, certain stipulations/ restrictions have been imposed for smooth and uninterrupted supply of power to the consumers across the State by all concerned of WBSEDCL upto $27^{\text{th}}$ March 2020 vide this Office Circular No. 2/2020 dated 23.03.2020.

Now in view of the order bearing no. H&FW/131/20 dated 24.03.2020 of the Chief Secretary, Govt. of West Bengal, it is further notified for information to all concerned that the stipulations/restrictions as imposed vide this Office Circular No. 2/2020 dated 23.03.2020 shall be continued throughout the entire license area of WBSEDCL upto $31^{\mathrm{st}}$ March 2020.
