Circular No: 6/2020

Dated: 13.04.2020

# Sub: Preventive measures to contain the spread of COVID-19

In view of notification of "Complete Safety Restrictions" issued vide Order No. H&FW/120/20 dated 22.03.2020 and the Order No. H&FW/131/20 dated 24.03.2020 both of the Chief Secretary, Govt. of West Bengal in the urban and rural area of the State as mentioned therein, certain stipulations/ restrictions have been imposed for smooth and uninterrupted supply of power to the consumers across the State by all concerned of WBSEDCL upto $14^{\text{th}}$ April 2020 vide this Office Circular No. 5/2020 dated 31.03.2020.

Now it is further notified for information to all concerned that the stipulations/restrictions imposed vide this Office Circulars No. 2/2020 dated 23.03.2020, 4/2020 dated 27.03.2020 and 05/2020 dated 31.03.2020 is hereby extended throughout the entire license area of WBSEDCL upto $30^{\mathrm{th}}$ April 2020 subject to the following conditions:-

1) Considering the present precarious financial condition of the company due to very poor revenue collection at CCCs during this unprecedented lockdown throughout the State, it is felt that apart from operation and maintenance works for smooth supply of powers to the consumers, bare minimum scale of employees may be maintained at CCCs, Divisions, Regions, Zones so that some essential activities of those offices may be kept in operation.   
2) For providing a safe and hygienic working environment, the staff attending offices may be provided with appropriate Personal Protective Equipments as may be required by the local authority.   
3) Physical distancing of employees as well as consumers has to be ensured by physically marking on the floor specially in front of the cash counters.   
4) To maintain the bare minimum scale of the employees at the respective offices, the controlling officers may prepare rosters of employees and the pick-up and drop facility through departmental vehicles shall be ensured for those employees for performance of their duties smoothly.

All other terms and conditions of Circular No. 2/2020 dated 23.03.2020 will remain unchanged.
