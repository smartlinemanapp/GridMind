Circular No. - 10/2020

Date:15.06.2020

Keeping in view of the memorandum issued through memo No. 218-CS/ 2020 dated 30.05.2020 of the Chief Secretary, Govt. of West Bengal and subsequent memorandum issued vide memo No.1912-F(H) dated 09.06.2020 and 1921-F(H) dated 10.06.2020 of the Additional Chief Secretary, Finance Department, Government of West Bengal and in continuation to this office Circular No. 09/2020 dated 06.06.2020, in the context of prevailing conditions in respect of COVID-19 in the State, after careful consideration it is, hereby, notified to all concerned of the Company to ensure attendance of $50\%$ strength of employees on every working day on rotation basis in the offices from the Hq. up to the level of Zones with effect from $16^{\mathrm{th}}$ June 2020 with following time schedule:-

1) The attendance of Officers and staff up to the rank of Superintending Engineer or equivalent will be staggered - on each day $25\%$ from 09.30 A.M. to 2.30 P.M. and the other $25\%$ from 12.30 P.M. 5.30 P.M so that attendance of $50\%$ strength of such category of officials can be ensured on every day.   
2) For the officers in the rank of Addl. Chief Engineer or equivalent, Heads of different Offices, there will be no such staggering of working hours and they are to attend offices on all working days.   
3) The offices of the Regional Managers, Project Managers (RE), Divisional Managers and Station Managers including Divisional Stores and DCCs are to be maintained with $50\%$ staff on normal working hours for which roster may be made for attendance of employees on alternate working days.   
4) For the offices and plants of Hydel Projects, ALDC, Data Centres, Substations which normally operate on shift basis will continue to operate as was done earlier for which concerned authorities will take care regarding maintenance of social distancing.   
5) However, Controlling Officers, may arrange to bring one or a few officials by making transport arrangement for them on any or a few days, if felt necessary, who have to perform specific and urgent duties during the full working hours of the day.

The Controlling Officers/respective Heads of the Offices will prepare roster of duties for the employees required to attend offices on different shifts on alternate dates for smooth operation and functioning.

The following guidelines for ensuring the safety and well being of officers and staff to contain the spread of COVID-19 in the workplace shall also be followed in all offices of the Company:-

a) Only asymptomatic officers and staff shall be allowed to attend offices on rotation basis. Anyone with any sort of symptom like mild fever, cough and cold need not attend office.   
b) Officers and staff residing in containment zone shall not come to office. He/she will work from home till the area is declared as a Clear Area (category C).   
c) Attendance of officers and staff who share a common sitting place should not exceed 10 on rotation basis. The sitting arrangements must be made in such a way that the distance between the two desks should not be less than 2.0 metres. In case it is not possible to maintain the distance of 2.0 metres with the presence of 10 persons in a common area/enclosure, the number of officer and staff should be further reduced. Roster should be prepared accordingly.   
d) Officers/staff who will not attend office because of roster arrangement as stated above, shall be advised to be available on telephone and/or any other electronics media so that they may be contacted as and when required.   
e) While in office premises, wearing of face mask at all times, frequent hand washing, use of hand sanitizer and adherence to other safety measures as notified earlier shall be strictly followed. Any case of non-wearing of masks shall be seriously viewed.   
f) Sitting arrangements of visitors shall also be made at a distance of at least 2.0 metres.   
g) Officers/staff will disinfect their personal equipments like keyboards, mouse, phone, AC remote, etc. themselves as frequently as possible.   
h) Arrangements shall be made for disinfection of frequently touched places such as electric switches, door knobs, washroom fixtures, elevator switches etc. Arrangements should also be made for total sanitization of the entire office premises at regular intervals.   
i) Face to face meetings/discussion should be avoided as far as possible. Officers and staff will use intercom/phone/VC for interactions.   
j) In the lifts meant for common use, not more than 3(three) persons shall be allowed to take entry at a time.

This order shall be effective from $16^{\text{th}}$ June 2020 and will remain in force up to $1^{\text{st}}$ July 2020.
