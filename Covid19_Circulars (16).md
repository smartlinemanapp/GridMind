Circular No. - 16/2020

Dated: 31-07-2020

In continuation to this Office Circulars no. 10/2020 dated 15.06.2020 and 11/2020 dated 30.06.2020, it is further notified for information to all concerned that the stipulations/restrictions imposed vide above stated circulars are, hereby, extended up to 31st August 2020.

However, at the time of preparation of roster it should be ensured that the overall attendance of officers and staff taken together shall not exceed $50\%$ of the total strength of the officials posted at that office on any particular day.

All other terms and conditions of the said Circular shall, however, remain unchanged.

Further, the stipulations/guidelines as imposed vide this Office Circular No. 14/2020 dated 21.07.2020 shall also remain in force.
