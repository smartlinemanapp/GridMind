Sub:Quarantine Leave for home isolation in mild/asymptomatic cases of COVID-19

As per revised guidelines dated 05.01.2022 issued by the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Government of India, Quarantine Leave will be granted by the Controlling Officer on the Positive(+ve) Test Report of 'COVID-19' for a period of 7 (seven) days, from the date of sampling, for home isolation in mild/asymptomatic cases to the employee concerned or in the family or household of an employee. Any Leave necessary for Quarantine purpose in excess of 7 (seven) days shall be treated as ordinary leave. As per said guideline, there is no need for re-testing after the home isolation period is over.

The above guidelines shall be followed only for home isolation in mild/asymptomatic cases of COVID-19 for the present and will remain valid until further order.
