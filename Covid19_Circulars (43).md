Circular No: CHR11

Dated: 2010312020

In the event of global outbreak of Novel Coronavirus (COVID-19), certain precautionary measures as per the guideline of Department of Health and Family Welfare, Govt. of West Bengal are to be followed by the Controlling Officers/Head of Offices as well as the employees of the company who or whose family members were abroad on tour and are returning to the country during this period.

In this regard the following advisory of the Govt. Of West Bengal may please be referred to:

"Since both the COVID-19 positive cases in West Bengal had got infected abroad and carried the viral infection into Kolkata, the Health & Family Welfare Dept, Govt. Of West Bengal appeals to all persons, who have returned from abroad in the recent past, specially from UK, USA, Europe and the gulf countries to necessarily confine themselves in home quarantine for a period of 14 days. Social distancing measures are a must for the containment of COVID-19"

Accordingly, it is notified for all concerned to visit the website of the Department of Health and Family Welfare, Govt. of West Bengal (https://www.wbhealth.gov.in/) regularly for the latest guidelines issued for the purpose and to follow the said guidelines for the employees including their family members returning from abroad before resuming their duties. In case of any query/ necessity, the office of the CMOH/ District Magistrate of the concerned Districts may also be contacted for guidance.
