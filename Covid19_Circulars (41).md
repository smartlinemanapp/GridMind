Sub: Regularization of attendance record and its related transactions in SAP ERP System for the period on & from $16^{\text{th}}$ April, 2021 to $15^{\text{th}}$ July, 2021

With the objective to contain the spread of Covid-19 in the State certain stipulations restrictions were enforced by the State Government on and from 19.04.2021. Accordingly the Company has also regulated its business activities by issuance of necessary Circulars periodically maintaining parity with the Govt. guidelines (refer Circular Nos.: CHR/01 dtd. 19.04.21, CHR/02 dtd. 30.04.21, CHR/03 dtd. 16.05.21, Circular No./06 dtd. 21.05.21, CHR/04 dtd. 24.05.21, CHR/05 dtd. 28.05.2021, CHR/06 dtd. 29.05.21, CHR/07 dtd. 04.06.21, CHR/08 dtd. 15.06.21, CHR/09 dtd. 30.06.21, CHR/10 dtd. 05.07.21, CHR/11 dtd. 29.07.21 & CHR/12 dtd. 31.07.21).

By virtue of this, the Company has also relaxed the norms on SAP ERP related activities (HCM Module) in the context of payroll processing of its employees by exempting the provision of recording of attendance record in SAP ERP System for three consecutive payroll months i.e. May 2021, June 2021 and July 2021 where salary was disbursed on provisional attendance basis.

Now, considering the present scenario the Company has relaxed the restrictions w.e.f. 02.08.2021 and accordingly we have to regularize the payroll related activities which were kept pending so long through uploading of attendance records (16.04.2021 to 15.07.2021). regularization of the absence period and performing its related transactions like disbursement of Overtime Allowance (OTA). Night Shift Allowance (NSA), etc. for the payroll month from May 2021 to July 2021, together with transactions pertaining to the month of August 2021 payroll, so that, all related adjustment in payment/leave may be settled along with the August 2021 salary. All the aforesaid pending activities need be completed before executing leave accrual programme for the period from 01.07.2021 to 31.12.2021.

In view of the above, the following modalities have been framed towards regularization of absence period in the following manner for ready reference:

Any other scenario, which is not covered above, shall be dealt with on case-to-case basis, depending upon its merit.

It is to be ensured that all kind of additional payments / deductions, if any, is to be effected to the employees as the case may be during disbursement of August 2021 Salary.

Usual course of payroll related activities shall be followed w.e.f 16.07.2021 in HCM Module without any deviation.

All concerned are advised to follow the above guidelines scrupulously to regularize the period of absence during the COVID-19 restrictions period appropriately in the HCM Module under SAP ERP System.
