Circular No. -15/2020.

Dated: 22.07.2020

In terms of the Govt. Order issued vide memo no. 283-CS/2020 dated 21.07.2020 of the Chief Secretary, Government of West Bengal, state wide lockdown shall be observed on Thursday, $23^{\mathrm{rd}}$ July 2020, Saturday $25^{\mathrm{th}}$ July 2020 and Wednesday, $29^{\mathrm{th}}$ July 2020 from 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. on each day.

Keeping in view on the above and in continuation to this Office Circular No. 14/2020 dated 21.07.2020, it is further notified for information of all concerned that the offices of the company will function in the manner as mentioned herein below on $23^{\text{rd}}$ July 2020, $25^{\text{th}}$ July 2020 and $29^{\text{th}}$ July 2020:

1) Bare minimum scales of employees are to be maintained in CCCs, Divisions, Regions, Zones including Stores, etc. which are not falling within the broader containment zones for smooth supply of power to the consumers   
2) $33 / 11\mathrm{KV}$ Sub-Stations will operate as per the existing schedule with the manpower as may be required for such operation   
3) Employees attached to ALDC in shift operation shall continue to perform as per normal roaster   
4) Employees attached to Hydel Power Station/PPSP in operation and maintenance duty shall continue to perform their duty as per roster in existence   
5) RE Projects are to be closed on those days   
6) In Corporate H.Q. at Vidyut Bhawan, the offices dealing with non-emergent nature of issues may remain closed for those days and remaining offices will be maintained with bare minimum employees only for which concerned Directors will take necessary decision.

Those who are not coming to offices because of non-inclusion in roster within the segment of bare minimum will also have to make themselves available through telephones/mobile and electronics mode of communications. If required, they should also make themselves available in the office subject to provision of transportation services.
