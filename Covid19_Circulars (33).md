# Circular No: CHR/07

# Dated: 04.06.2021

In continuation to this Office Circular No. CHR/03 dated 16.05.2021 read with Circular No. CHR/06 dated 29.05.2021 it is, hereby, notified to all concerned that the RE Project Offices which have been closed for the time being in terms of the said circular, shall start functioning with bare minimum employees as may be required for smooth operation and functions with immediate effect.

However, since mobilization of workforce for operation and maintenance by keeping the Sub-stations, CCCs and Stores open is still a challenging task, considering the present restrictions in public transport, all the vehicles of the RE Projects, spared to the Divisions, cannot be released immediately to report to the Project Managers, RE Projects except one vehicle as per choice of the Project Manager with immediate effect.

In partial modification of above mentioned Circulars, it is, hereby, further notified for information of all concerned that all the Chief Engineers, General Managers, Additional Chief Engineers, Additional General Managers and the officers in the equivalent rank presently posted at Head Quarter will have to attend offices on all working days in normal working hours with immediate effect.

However, all other terms and conditions of this Circular No. CHR/03 dated 16.05.2021 and CHR/06 dated 29.05.2021 will remain unchanged.
