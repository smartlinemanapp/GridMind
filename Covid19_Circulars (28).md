Now in view of Order dated 15/01/2022 issued vide No. 753/XX-ISS/2M-22/2020 of the Chief Secretary, Govt. of West Bengal, it is further notified for information of all concerned that the stipulations/restrictions imposed vide this Office Circular No. CHR/01 dated 03.01.2022 is, hereby, extended throughout the entire license area of WBSEDCL up to 31<sup>st</sup> January 2022.
