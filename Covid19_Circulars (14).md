Circular No. - 14/2020

Dated: 21-07-2020

In continuation to this Office Circular no. 11/2020 dated 30.06.2020, it is further notified for information to all concerned that all the offices of the company located within the broader containment zones shall remain closed during the period of lockdown as per notification of respective District Administrations.

However, 33/11 KV Sub-Stations, ALDC, Power Generating units, etc. which fall within the broader containment zones, if any, will operate with bare minimum manpower to maintain smooth generation and distribution of power to the consumers. The LT and HT mobile vans of respective areas also will function for maintenance and restoration of power supply to the consumers.

All other terms and conditions of the said Circular shall, however, remain unchanged.
