# Sub: Guidelines on Preventive Measures to Contain Spread of COVID-19 pandemic

In continuation to this Office Circulars No. CHR/01 dated 19.04.2021 and CHR/02 dated 30.04.2021 and in view of the Order dated 15.05.2021 issued vide No. 647-ISS/2M-22/2020 of the Chief Secretary, Govt. of West Bengal notifying certain restrictions w.e.f. 6 AM of 16th May 2021 to 6 PM of 30th May 2021 throughout the State as mentioned therein, the following stipulations/restrictions have been decided for further necessary action by all concerned:-

1) Bare minimum scales of employees are to be maintained in CCCs, Divisions, Regions, Zones including Stores, etc. as per roster for smooth supply of power. Non-emergent nature of activities may remain suspended for the time being.   
2) 33/11 KV Sub-Stations will operate as per the existing schedule with the manpower as may be required for such operation.   
3) RE project Offices are to be closed for the time being and vehicles engaged for RE Project Offices have to be asked to report to the Divisional Managers of the adjacent Divisions for facilitation of transportation of the staff of the CCCs and Sub-Stations as may be required. Regional Managers are requested to finalize the allotment of such vehicles within different Divisions on the need basis.   
4) Employees attached to Hydel Power Stations/ PPSP site in operation and maintenance duty shall continue to perform their duty as per the normal roster.   
5) Employees attached to ALDC in shift operation shall continue to perform their duty as per the normal roster.   
6) In corporate H.Q. at Vidyut Bhavan, the offices dealing with non-emergent nature of issues may remain closed for the time being and the remaining offices will be maintained with bare minimum staff only for which concerned Directors will take necessary decision.   
7) All the consumers are to be requested and encouraged to pay their electricity bills through different electronic modes of payment only as already introduced by the Company instead of depositing cash at CCC's cash counters.   
8) To maintain the bare minimum scale of employees at the respective offices, the Controlling Officers/Divisional Managers/Station Managers may prepare a roster of employees and the pick-up and drop facility through departmental vehicle, within a reasonable distance, shall have to be ensured for those employees for performing of their duties smoothly. However, Such transportation facilities should be provided in a restricted manner.

9) Employees deployed for duty during this emergent situation may be requested to carry their Identity Cards along with them, which may be displayed on their body to enable the Civil & Police authorities to recognize them easily.   
10) Outsourced employees and Part Time Sweepers may also be provided with an Identity Card/Identification document so that they may not found any difficulty to attend their workplaces.   
11) Physical distancing of employees as well as consumers has to be ensured by physical marking on the floor specially in front of the cash counters.

Those who are not coming to the offices because of non-inclusion in the roster will also make themselves available through telephones/mobiles and/or any other electronic mode of communications. If required, they should also make themselves available in the office subject to provision of transportation support.

This arrangement will continue upto $30^{\text{th}}$ May 2021.
