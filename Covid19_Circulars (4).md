# Sub: Preventive measures to contain the spread of COVID-19

In view of notification of "Complete Safety Restrictions" issued vide Order No. H&FW/120/20 dated 22.03.2020 and the Order No. H&FW/131/20 dated 24.03.2020 both of the Chief Secretary, Govt. of West Bengal in the urban and rural area of the State as mentioned therein, certain stipulations/ restrictions have been imposed for smooth and uninterrupted supply of power to the consumers across the State by all concerned of WBSEDCL upto 31st March 2020 vide this Office Circular No. 4/2020 dated 27.03.2020.

Now it is further notified for information to all concerned that the stipulations/restrictions imposed vide this Office Circular No. 4/2020 dated 27.03.2020 is hereby extended throughout the entire license area of WBSEDCL upto $14^{\text{th}}$ April 2020.
