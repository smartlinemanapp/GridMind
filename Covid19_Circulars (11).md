Office Order: No. 10

Date: 12.03.2021.

Consequent upon wide spread containment of Novel Corona Virus (Covid'19) and subsequent Lockdown effected on & from 24.03.2020 to 15.06.2020 at a stretch and intermittently for a few days thereafter most of the establishment of WBSEDCL could not function fully throughout the year 2020. As a result, the number of working days for the Calendar Year 2020 has substantially been reduced than that of previous Calendar Years.

Now to award promotion to the employees eligible for the same in the Calendar year 2021 onwards, assessment of their Attendance in a scale of 15 marks for the preceding Calendar Year, i.e. 2020 and/or preceding 2(Two) Calendar Years, as the case may be, in terms of Cl-B of Office Order No-S/1396 dt. 08.10.2001, read with Cl-2 of Office Order NoP/125 dt. 21.06.2002 and Cl. 5(b)(ii) of Promotion Policy 2010, is one of the qualifying criteria to assess their suitability for such promotion.

Keeping a close look on the overall impact of such complete & partial Lockdown imposed in view the unprecedented (Covid-19) Pandemic in the Calendar Year 2020 and to facilitate uniform assessment of marks of Attendance of the employees during said Calendar Year for such pool of employees (upto Class-I, $3^{\mathrm{rd}}$ level) eligible for promotion, it is therefore enjoined upon all concerned that number of Working Days for Calendar Year 2020 shall be considered minimum 122 (One hundred and twenty-two) days or the actual number of days for which the employee concerned was assigned/asked for duty, whichever is higher.

This is for necessary guidance & compliance of all concerned.
