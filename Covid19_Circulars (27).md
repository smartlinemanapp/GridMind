# Sub:- Guidelines on Preventive measures to contain spread of COVID-19 pandemic

Whereas, to contain the spread of COVID-19 pandemic second wave, the attendance of the employees in any office/establishment has been reduced to $50\%$ on any given day vide Circular no.: CHR/01 dated 19.04.2021; AND

Whereas, it is a necessary requirement at various offices/establishments to continue various essential activities related to COVID-19 pandemic, submission of different reports/returns of emergent nature, timely payments of bills of the vendors, completion of different financial report/returns, etc in a time bound manner; AND

Whereas, the above activities at different offices/establishment could not be taken-up timely in a full-fledged manner due to scarcity of manpower on any given day in the office/establishments; AND

Whereas, there are a lot of compelling issues which are required to be dealt with by WBSEDCL being an emergency service provider, specially during this ongoing second wave of COVID-19 pandemic;

It is, hereby, notified for information to all concerned that the Controlling Officers of the respective offices may advise/ask any officer/staff under his control to attend office to meet up the emergent requirement, as and when needed, when the employee concerned is not in roster duty for the day.

If the concerned employee is unable to attend office as per the advice of the Controlling Officer, without any valid reason, the employee will have to submit necessary leave application for that day which will be debited from his leave account, subject to approval of the Controlling Officer.

Provision of this order may be invoked on an emergent and on felt need basis.

This order is issued in continuation of this office Circular No.; CHR/01 dated 19.04.2021 and all other terms of the said order shall, however, remain unchanged.
