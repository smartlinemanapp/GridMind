Circular No: 07/2020

Dated: 30.04.2020

# Sub: Preventive measures to contain the spread of COVID-19

In view of the notification of "Complete Safety Restrictions" issued vide Order No. H&FW/120/20 dated 22.03.2020 and the Order No. H&FW/131/20 dated 24.03.2020 both of the Chief Secretary, Govt. of West Bengal in respect of the urban and rural areas of the State as mentioned therein, certain stipulations/ restrictions have been imposed for smooth and uninterrupted supply of power and consumer services throughout the entire license area of WBSEDCL up to $30^{\text{th}}$ April 2020 vide this Office Circular No. 6/2020 dated 13.04.2020 read with Circular No. 2/2020 dated 23.03.2020.

Now it is further notified for information to all concerned that the stipulations/ restrictions imposed vide this office Circular No-6/2020 dated 13.04.2020 are herby extended until further order.
