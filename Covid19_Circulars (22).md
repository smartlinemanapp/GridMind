# Sub: Preventive measures to contain the spread of COVID-19

In view of the unprecedented situation arising out of outbreak of Corona virus (COVID-19) and apprehension of its spread through human contact and in line with the recent orders of the Govt of West Bengal in this regard, it has been felt necessary to minimize the gathering at different offices of the company without affecting the consumer services. Accordingly, it has been decided that the following steps are to be taken by all the controlling officers:

i) Emphasis is to be given for reduction of deployment of manpower by $50\%$ at each office on weekly rotational basis by the Controlling Officers of respective offices. In case of any difficulty for such weekly rotation of manpower, it should be made on a half weekly basis i.e. $50\%$ of the employees should be deployed on the first 3(three) days of the week and rest $50\%$ of the employees should be deployed for the next 3(three) days. If this system is also not possible, then $50\%$ employees should be put on duty on every alternate day.   
ii) Head of the Office and the Controlling Officers should be on duty in the office regularly for the present. In case the Head of the Office/ Controlling Officer requires the presence of certain category of personnel whose service is required for emergent purposes of the public service delivery or closure of the financial year then exception may be made to point No.1 for reasons to be recorded in writing specifically in the roster.   
iii) All officers who are provided with departmental vehicles should be on duty in the office regularly for the present.   
iv) If the situation is such that only one employee is deployed for any job, like cash counter of CCC, cash counter at Region Office, Stores, DCC etc. that particular employee will be deployed for all the days for the present to ensure uninterrupted public service delivery and smooth closing of the financial year. However, such compelling circumstances is required to be recorded specifically by the Controlling Officer/ Head of the Office in the roster.   
v) Duty roster of all Customer Care Centers is to be ensured by the Divisional Managers & Regional Managers so that they are aware of availability of staff at each establishment/shift subject to point No.1 above.   
vi) The employees who would not attend the office due to introduction of such roster shall have to make themselves available on telephone and/or electronic means of

communication at all times. They should attend office if called for in the exigency of work.

vii) All the employees attached to Hydel Power Station/PPSP in operation and maintenance duty shall continue to perform their duty as per present roster in existence.   
viii) All employees attached to ALDC in shift operation shall continue to perform as per the normal roster.   
ix) All offices shall be properly cleaned and sanitized regularly. Everybody is advised to use sanitizers before taking entry into the office premises.   
x) All the employees are again advised to practice hand washing frequently while in office.   
xi) Entry of visitors in all offices should be restricted as far as practicable. Where it is necessary to allow visitors into the office premises, he/she should be requested to sanitize himself/herself using sanitizer before taking entry to the office premises.

This arrangement will continue upto $31^{\text{st}}$ March, 2020.
