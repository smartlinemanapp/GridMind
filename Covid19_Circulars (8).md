Circular No. - 09/2020

Date: 06.06.2020

In view of the huge devastation caused to the power distribution network by the Cyclone AMPHAN on 20.05.2020 evening,

# And

In view of the restoration activities of the ravaged electrical network going on in full swing in the affected districts which are in turn being aided and assisted by other districts in which a huge Nos. of workforce are involved day and night,

# And

In view of the Memo No. 218-CS/ 2020 dated 30.05.2020 of the Chief Secretary, Govt. of West Bengal, in the context of prevailing conditions in respect of COVID-19 in the State,

It is, hereby, ordered that the stipulations imposed vide this office Circular No. 6/2020 dated 13.04.2020 read with Circulars No. 07/2020 dated 30.04.2020 and Circulars No. 08/2020 dated 05.05.2020 are hereby extended up to 15th June 2020.
