Circular No: CHR/13

Dated: 06.04.2020

Sub: Exemption to the employees to mark Biometric Attendance system of the Company

Keeping in view of the Novel Coronavirus (COVID-19) outbreak in the country, the employees of the Company were exempted to mark their attendance in Bio-metric Attendance System of the Company upto $31^{\text{st}}$ March 2020 as a precautionary measure vide this Office Circular No. 10 dated 18.03.2020.

Now it is further notified for information to all concerned that the stipulations/restrictions imposed vide this Office Circular No. 10 dated 18.03.2020 will be continued until further order.
