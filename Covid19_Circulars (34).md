Circular No. CHR/08

Dated: 15.06.2021

Sub: Guidelines on Preventive Measures to Contain Spread of COVID-19 pandemic

Keeping in view of the Order dated 15.05.2021 issued vide No. 647-ISS/2M-22/2020 and Order dated 29.05.2021 issued vide no. 707-ISS/2M-/22/2020 both of the Chief Secretary, Govt. of West Bengal notifying certain restrictions throughout the State as mentioned therein, certain restrictions have been imposed vide this Office Circular No. CHR/03 dated 16.05.2021 read with this Office Circular No. CHR/06 dated 29.05.2021 for keeping the office open with bare minimum strength of staff for smooth and uninterrupted supply of power to the consumers across the State by all concerned of WBSEDCL up to $15^{\text{th}}$ June 2021.

Further, in continuation to stipulations/restrictions imposed vide aforesaid circulars dated 16.05.2021 and 29.05.2021 respectively, some additional guidelines have also been issued vide this Office Circular No. CHR/07 dated 04.06.2021.

Now, in view of Order dated 14.06.2021 issued vide No. 753-ISS/2M-22/2020 of the Chief Secretary, Govt. of West Bengal, it is further notified for information of all concerned that the restrictions imposed vide this Office Circular Nos. CHR/06 dated 29.05.2021 and CHR/07 dated 04.06.2021 are, hereby, extended throughout the entire license area of WBSEDCL up to 6 PM of $30^{\text{th}}$ June 2021.
