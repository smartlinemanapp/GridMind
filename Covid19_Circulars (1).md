# Sub: Preventive measures to contain the spread of COVID-19

In continuation to our Circular No. 1820 dated 21.03.2020 and in view of the Order dated 22.03.2020 issued vide No. H&FW/120/20 of the Chief Secretary, Govt. of West Bengal notifying "Complete Safety Restrictions" w.e.f. 16.00 hrs of 23.03.2020 to 24.00 hrs of 27.03.2020 in the urban and rural area of the State as mentioned therein, the following stipulations/ restrictions have been decided for further necessary action by all concerned:-

i) Bare minimum scales of employees are to be maintained in CCCs, Divisions, Regions, Zones including Stores, etc. as per roster for smooth supply of power only. Non-emergent nature of activities may remain suspended for the time being.   
ii) $33 / 11\mathrm{KV}$ Sub-stations will operate as per the existing schedule with the manpower as may be required for such operation.   
iii) RE Project offices are to be closed for the time being and vehicles engaged at RE Project offices have to be asked to report to the Divisional Mangers of the adjacent division offices for facilitations of transportations of the staff of the CCCs and Sub-Stations as may be required.   
iv) Employees attached to Hydel Power Station/PPSP in operation and maintenance duty shall continue to perform their duty as per roster in existence.   
v) Employees attached to ALDC in shift operation shall continue to perform as per the normal roster.   
vi) In Corporate H.Q. at Vidyut Bhawan, the offices dealing with non-emergent nature of issues may remain closed for the time being and the remaining offices will be maintained with bare minimum staff only for which concerned Directors will take necessary action.   
vii) All the Consumers are to be requested and encouraged to pay their electricity bills through different electronic modes of payment only as already introduced by the company instead of deposition of cash at CCCs cash counter.

Those who are not coming to offices because of non-inclusion in roster will also have to make themselves available through telephones/mobile and electronics mode of communications. If required, they should also make himself available in the office subject to provision of transportation support.

This arrangement will continue upto $27^{\mathrm{th}}$ March, 2020.
