Sub: Guidelines on Preventive Measures to Contain Spread of COVID-19 pandemic

Keeping in view of the Order dated 02.01.2022 issued vide No. 753/XVII-ISS/2M-22/2020 of the Chief Secretary, Govt. of West Bengal and subsequent Order dated 15.01.2022 vide no. 753/XX-ISS/2M-22/2020 of the Chief Secretary, Govt. of West Bengal notifying certain restrictions w.e.f. 02.01.2022 to 31.01.2022 throughout the State as mentioned therein, certain stipulations/restrictions have been imposed for smooth and uninterrupted supply of power to the consumers across the State by all concerned of WBSEDCL up to $31^{\mathrm{st}}$ January 2022 vide this Office Circular No. CHR/03 dated 15.01.2022.

Now in view of Order dated 31/01/2022 issued vide No. 753/XXII-ISS/2M-22/2020 of the Chief Secretary, Govt. of West Bengal and in partial modification of Office Circular No. CHR/03 dated 15.01.2022 it is further notified for information of all concerned that the attendance in any office/establishment shall not exceed $75\%$ on any given day. The employees will attend office on a rotational basis as may be decided by the Head of the Office/Controlling Officer by framing a roster of duty.

All other terms and conditions of Circular No. CHR/03 dated 15.01.2022 will, however, remain unchanged.

This order takes effect from 01.02.2022 and will remain in force till 15.02.2022.
