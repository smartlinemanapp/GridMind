Circular No. CHR/12

Dated: 31.07.2021

Sub: Ensuring Normal Attendance in Office on all Working Days w.e.f. 02.08.02021

Keeping in view of the para - 9 of the Order vide No. 753-III-ISS/2M-22/2020 dated 14.07.2021 of the Chief Secretary, Govt. of West Bengal and in view of the Order No.: 294-POW-11099/9/2020-SECTION(POWER) dated 30.07.2021 of the Additional Chief Secretary, Department of Power, Govt. of West Bengal, it has been decided in the interest of public service as well as of the smooth performance of the works of this Organisation that all the officers and staff, including those engaged on contractual basis shall attend office on all working days in regular manner, in usual office hours w.e.f. 02.08.2021.

This order is issued in supersession of this office's previous circulars, so far the attendance in office is concerned and for information and necessary action by all concerned.
