Circular No. - 18/2020

Dated: 04.08.2020

In partial modification of this Office Circular No. 17/2020 dated 31.07.2020, it is further notified for information of all concerned that in terms of Govt. Order issued vide memo no. 287-CS/2020 dated 03.08.2020 of the Chief Secretary, Govt. of West Bengal, state wide lockdown shall be observed on Wednesday 5<sup>th</sup> August, Saturday 8<sup>th</sup> August, Thursday 20<sup>th</sup> August, Friday 21<sup>st</sup> August, Thursday 27<sup>th</sup> August, Friday 28<sup>th</sup> August and Monday 31<sup>st</sup> August 2020 and accordingly the offices of the company will function in the manner as already specified in this Office Circular no. 15/2020 dated 22.07.2020 on the abovementioned dates of statewide lockdown.

Further, it is notified for information of all concerned that in case of rescheduling of any of the abovementioned dates regarding state wide lockdown by the Govt. of West Bengal, the offices of the company will function in the manner as already specified in this Office Circular no. 15/2020 dated 22.07.2020 on those re-scheduled date(s) to observe the state wide lockdown in terms of Govt. Order.
