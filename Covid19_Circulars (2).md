Sub: Movement of Manpower during Lockdown period for smooth maintenance of power supply

It has been brought to the notice of the management that sometimes the employees deployed for maintenance of power supply are facing difficulties in their movement from residence/office to respective work places in spite of our best efforts.

In a few cases it has been reported that officials/workers engaged in Sub-Stations are finding it difficult to remain at worksite continuously for a very long period and some others are finding it difficult to report for duty at Sub-station/Mobile Van/CCC for want of suitable transport services.

Under these circumstances, the Divisional Managers/Station Managers are, hereby, requested to facilitate movement of such officials/workers by arranging their transport through official vehicles where needed, enabling them to report for duty effectively as much as possible.

In this regard it may be noted that for facilitation of unrestricted movement of on-duty employees of Electricity and other sectors an order was issued vide No. 27-CS/2020 dated 25.03.2020 of the Chief Secretary, Govt. of West Bengal (copy enclosed).

Hence, it is advised that all the employees deployed for performance of duty during this emergent situation in view of outbreak of COVID-19 may be requested to carry their Identity Cards along with them which may be displayed on their body to enable the Civil & Police authorities to recognize them easily.

Outsourced employees and PTSs may be provided with an Identity Card / Identification document so that they may not find any difficulty to attend their work places.

This is for information and necessary action to all concerned.
