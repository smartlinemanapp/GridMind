Office Circular No. CHR/01

Dated: 19.04.2021

Sub: Guidelines on Preventive Measures to Contain Spread of COVID-19 pandemic

Keeping in view of the ongoing spread of COVID-19 pandemic second wave, the following guidelines, shall strictly be ensured and followed to contain the spread of COVID-19 in the workplaces in all offices of the Company:

1) The attendance in any office/establishment shall not exceed $50\%$ on any given day. The employees will attend office on a rotational basis as may be decided by the Head of the Office/Controlling Officer by framing a roster of duty. However, officers in the rank of Addl. Chief Engineer, Addl. General Manager, Dy. Chief Engineer, Dy. General Manager and/or of equivalent rank and above have to attend office regularly.   
2) However, Officers holding independent charge as Head of an office like Regional Manager, Project Manager, Divisional Manager, Station Manager and similar others have to attend office regularly irrespective of their rank without any rotation.   
3) Officials who will not have to attend office physically on any working day, has to be available at his residence during normal office hours and have to remain connected by phone and/or other means of connectivity so that his/her service may be utilized as and when required.   
4) The employees attached to ALDC in shift operation, $33 / 11\mathrm{KV}$ Sub-stations and Hydel Power Stations/ PPSP shall continue to perform their duty as per normal roster.   
5) The LT and HT mobile vans of respective areas will function for maintenance and restoration of power supply to the consumers.   
(6) Entrance of all offices should be provided with soaps or sanitizer for mandatory hand hygiene. All office premises should be well ventilated as far as possible.   
7) While in office premises, wearing of face mask at all times, frequent hand washing, use of hand sanitizer and adherence to other safety measures as notified earlier shall be strictly followed. Any case of non-wearing of masks shall be viewed seriously.

8) Make sure that workplaces are clean and hygienic. Officers/staff will disinfect their personal equipments like keyboards, mouse, phone, AC remote etc. themselves as frequently as possible.   
9) Arrangements shall be made for disinfection of frequently touched surfaces such as electric switches, door knobs, washroom fixtures, elevator switches, etc. Arrangements should also be made for total sanitization of the entire office premises at regular intervals, i.e. once in a week.   
10) Face to face meetings/discussion should be avoided as far as possible. Officers and staff will use intercom/phone/VC for interactions as far as practicable.   
11) Number of people in the elevators/lifts shall be restricted. In the elevators/lifts meant for common use, not more than 3(three) persons shall be allowed to take entry at a time.   
12) Entry of visitors should be restricted as far as practicable. However, sitting arrangements of visitors shall also be made at a distance of at least 2.0 metres.   
13) Parisheba Mela, Safety Awareness Programme/Seminars, Training programme shall have to be restricted and be conducted on virtual mode.   
14) Physical distancing of consumers shall have to be ensured by physically marking on the floor specially in front of cash counters of Customer Care Centers.

This order takes immediate effect and shall continue until further order.
