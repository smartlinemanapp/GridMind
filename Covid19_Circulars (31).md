Circular No. CHR/05

Dated: 15.02.2022

Sub: Ensuring Normal Attendance in offices on all Working Days w.e.f. 16.02.2022

Keeping in view of the Order dated 14/02/2022 issued vide No. 753/XXIII-ISS/2M-22/2020 of the Chief Secretary, Govt. of West Bengal and in view of the Order dated 02.02.2022 issued vide no. 58-POW-11099/9/2020-SECTION(POWER) of the Additional Chief Secretary, Department of Power, Govt. of West Bengal, it has been decided in the interest of the public service as well as for smooth performance of the works of this Organization that all the officers and staff, including those engaged on contractual basis shall attend office on all working days in regular manner, in usual officer hours with effect from 16<sup>th</sup> February, 2022.

This order is issued in supersession of all the previous circulars issued in the respect of attendance in offices is concerned and for information and necessary action by all concerned.
