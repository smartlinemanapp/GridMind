Sub: Exemption to the employees to mark Biometric Attendance System of the Company

In view of the Novel Coronavirus (COVID-19) outbreak in the country and considering one of the common methods of transmission of Coronavirus being touching of infected surfaces, it has been decided to exempt the employees of the company from marking of attendance in Biometric Attendance System of the Company as a precautionary measure till $31^{\mathrm{st}}$ March 2020.

It is further advised that attendance of the employees from $19^{\text{th}}$ March, 2020 onwards will be registered manually in the conventional Attendance Register as was done before introduction of Biometric Attendance System. All employees are also requested to clearly indicate the time of arrival and time of departure under their signature in the Attendance Register.

This order is issued with the approval of Chairman & Managing Director, WBSEDCL.
