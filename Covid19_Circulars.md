# Sub: Preventive measures to be taken to contain the spread of Novel Coronavirus (COVID-19)

In order to contain Novel Coronavirus (COVID-19), certain preventive measures are advised to be taken by all the employees of the company for the safety of themselves and their families. Accordingly the following advisories are issued for the well being of the employees of the company and their families:

i) To maintain physical hygiene and physical distancing.

ii) Ensure proper training and frequent sanitization of the workplaces particularly the frequently touched surfaces.

iii) Practice frequent hand washing with soap and water or Alcohol based hand rub. Alcohol based Sanitizers/ Liquid Soap to be provided at the entry points and other convenient point of Offices/departments and also at all washrooms.

iv) Cover your nose and mouth with handkerchief / tissue papers while sneezing and coughing and throw the used tissue papers in bins immediately after use.

v) Use inner side of your elbow for sneezing and don't cough on the palm.

vi) Keep safe distance from persons during interactions.

vii) Avoid social and official gathering as far as practicable.

(viii) Pre-scheduled programmes at the Auditorium/Guest Houses, if any, to be deferred until further order.

ix) Booking of Holiday Homes will be suspended until further order

x) Revision of plan for LTC/HTC as far as practicable.

xi) To avoid tours until extremely necessary.

xii) Gymnasium at Vidyut Bhawan Ground floor will remain closed until further order.

xiii) All employees may be advised to take care of their own health and look out for respiratory symptoms/ fever and if feeling unwell, should visit Doctors/Medical Officers after informing their Reporting Officer.

xiv) Correspondence through email to be utilized to the extent possible in place of physical sending of files and letters.

xy) Avoid handshake with any person and don't touch your mouth, nose, eyes.

xvi) Don't spit in Public Places.

xvii) Avoid panic and rumours and be cautious.

xviii) Advise all employees who are at higher risk i.e older employees, pregnant employees and employees who have underlying medical conditions, to take extra precautions.

This is for information and necessary guidance of all concerned.
