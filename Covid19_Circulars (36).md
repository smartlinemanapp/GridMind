# Sub: Guidelines on Preventive Measures to Contain Spread of COVID-19 pandemic

Keeping in view of Order dated 14.07.2021 issued vide No. 753/III-ISS/2M-22/2020 of the Chief Secretary, Govt. of West Bengal, it is further notified for information of all concerned that the restrictions imposed vide this Office Circular No. CHR/09 dated 30.06.2021 are, hereby, extended throughout the entire license area of WBSEDCL up to $30^{\text{th}}$ July 2021 with the following amendments:-

1) As an utility of emergency and essential service and in view of increasing load of revenue monitoring, resources mobilization, customer service delivery, etc. it is, hereby, advised that all the offices should ensure $50\%$ attendance of the officers and staff of the respective offices on every working day by preparing a roster for duty. Head of the offices will prepare a roster accordingly.   
2) Officials who will not have to attend office physically on any working day, has to be available at his residence during normal office hours and have to remain connected by phone and/or other means of connectivity so that his/her service may be utilized as and when required.   
3) The Controlling Officers of the respective offices may advise/ask any officer/staff under his control to attend office to meet up the emergent requirement, as and when needed, when the employee concerned is not in roster duty for the day.

If the concerned employee is unable to attend office as per the advice of the Controlling Officer, without any valid reason, the employee will have to submit necessary leave application for that day which will be debited from his leave account, subject to approval of the Controlling Officer.

All other terms and conditions of the Circular mentioned above will remain unchanged and this order is effective from 16th July 2021.
