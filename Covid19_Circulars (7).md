Circular No: 08/2020

Dated: 05.05.2020

In continuation to this Office Circular No. 07/2020 dated 30.04.2020 it is, hereby, notified to the all concerned that the RE Project Offices which have been closed for the time being and located outside the containment zones shall start functioning with bare minimum employees as may be required for smooth operation and functions with immediate effect.

However, since the mobilization of workforce for operation and maintenance by keeping the Sub-stations, CCCs and Stores open is still a challenging task, not all the vehicles of the RE Projects, spared to the Divisions can be released immediately to report to the Project Managers, RE except one vehicle as per choice of the Project Manager with immediate effect.

However, all other terms and conditions of this Circular No. 07/2020 dated 30.04.2020 will remain unchanged.

05.05.2020. (Sujay Sarkar) Director (HR)
